Policies

The editorial and publishing policies that govern the American Journal of Research & Innovation. A concise summary of each is given below; detailed guidance is linked where available.

Standards

Our policies in brief

Publication ethics (COPE)

AJRI follows the principles and best-practice guidelines of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE). See our full publication ethics policy.

Conflict of interest

Authors, reviewers and editors must disclose any financial or non-financial interests that could influence the work. Editors with a conflict recuse themselves from handling the manuscript.

Plagiarism

All submissions are screened with similarity-detection software. Plagiarism, self-plagiarism and redundant publication are grounds for rejection or retraction.

Data availability

Authors are encouraged to share underlying data. Each article includes a data-availability statement describing how and where the data can be accessed.

Human & animal ethics

Research on human participants requires ethics-committee approval and informed consent, following the Declaration of Helsinki. Animal studies must meet applicable welfare standards and approvals.

Privacy

Personal data of authors, reviewers and readers is used only to operate the journal and is never sold. Patient identifying details are never published without explicit consent.

Copyright & licensing

Authors retain copyright of their work. Articles are published open access under a Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0) licence, permitting reuse with attribution.

Open access

Every article is immediately and permanently free to read, download and share worldwide, with no subscription or reader charges.

Corrections & retractions

Honest errors are corrected promptly and transparently. Work found to be unreliable or unethical is retracted with a clear, public notice following COPE guidance.

Questions about our policies?

For any query about editorial or publishing policy, contact the editorial office. We are committed to transparency and to a fair, ethical process for every author and reviewer.
