05 · Instructions for Authors

Instructions for authors

Everything you need before submitting. See the full Author Guidelines for detail.

Aims & scope

An international, multidisciplinary, open-access journal spanning medicine, engineering, education, business, environment and interdisciplinary innovation.

Manuscript preparation

Structured manuscript (title page, abstract, keywords, IMRaD, references), consistent English, tables and figures with captions.

Submission process

Submit a title page, an anonymized manuscript and completed declarations by email or form; you receive a manuscript ID within 48 hours.

Peer-review policy

Editorial screening followed by independent double-blind peer review before any decision.

Ethics & publication ethics

Ethics approval and informed consent for human-subject work; misconduct handled under COPE-aligned publication ethics.

Copyright & open access

Authors retain copyright; articles publish under CC BY 4.0 and are free to read, download and share worldwide.

Article Processing Charge (APC)

AJRI is fully open access. Any applicable APC is confirmed in writing at acceptance; waivers may be considered on request. Authors are never charged to submit or to have a manuscript reviewed.

Presentation Fees

  • Oral presentation — USD $235

  • Poster presentation — USD $195

07 · Policies

Editorial & ethics policies

AJRI follows recognised standards for research and publication integrity. See the full Policies page.

Publication ethics

Editorial conduct, authorship, corrections and misconduct handling aligned with COPE guidelines.

Conflict of interest

Authors, reviewers and editors disclose financial and non-financial competing interests.

Plagiarism

All submissions are screened; plagiarism, redundant publication and image manipulation are grounds for rejection.

Data availability

Authors are encouraged to share underlying data and include a data-availability statement.

Human & animal ethics

Human-subject research requires ethics approval and informed consent; animal research follows welfare standards.

Privacy

Personal data of authors, reviewers and participants is handled confidentially — see our privacy policy.
